# 1.0.6

## New

- Updates now look after themselves on Home: when a new version is available and you're on Wi-Fi, MusiX downloads it automatically and shows live progress in a banner at the top of Home, right above your song downloads. Pause, resume or cancel it there, and you'll be prompted to install the moment it's ready.

## Fixed

- The player no longer plays one song while showing another's title. After a track fails to load, playback no longer resumes the previous song behind the wrong title - pressing play now resolves and plays the song you're actually looking at.

## Notes

- Built with Flutter. All tests and analysis pass.
